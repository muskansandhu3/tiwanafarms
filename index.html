Tiwana Family & Agricultural Heritage

Rooted in the Soil Since Early 2000s.

“Our mission is simple: to honor the soil of Surrey, preserve agricultural integrity, and deliver unrivaled freshness from our farm to your family's table.”

Founded in the early 2000s by the Tiwana family in South Surrey along 168 Street, Tiwana Farms represents a seamless blend of traditional Punjabi agricultural heritage and modern culinary standards. What began with a deep passion for cultivating rich Fraser Valley soil has grown into one of Surrey's most cherished local farm destinations.

Every pasture egg gathered, every crop harvested, and every house marinated specialty prepared at Tiwana Farms is guided by the Tiwana family's unyielding dedication to quality, freshness, and community trust. We believe food should taste authentic, rich, and deeply connected to where it was grown.
